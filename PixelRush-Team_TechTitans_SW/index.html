DONOR APPRECIATION

Giving blood is a gift. We can help make the journey easier.

Donating is voluntary. Some participating blood centres may offer practical support for the visit.

↔

Help with transport costs

Where a participating blood centre allows it, eligible donors can ask about support for documented travel expenses. Check the centre's policy before travelling. This is never payment for blood.

♡

A welcome break

Water and light refreshments when the donation camp provides them.

✉

A thank-you to keep

Ask about a donation certificate or appreciation note from the blood centre.
